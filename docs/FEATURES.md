# Features: your own photo and video home

By Erik Adler. Version 1.1.0.

The Archive is a self-hosted replacement for the photo-sharing and memory-keeping part of Instagram. You run the CMS and keep the originals on your own storage. Start with the [beginner guide](BEGINNER-GUIDE.md).

## Browse years of memories

- A continuous reverse-chronological timeline with automatic older-post loading.
- Responsive gallery columns for desktop, tablet, and phone.
- Captions, exact dates, and milestone ages calculated from a configurable birth date.
- A dense **Thumbnail wall** showing every photo and video, including every carousel item.
- **Jump to year** to find older memories quickly.
- Virtualized thumbnail rows and lazy static previews to limit rendering and video load.
- Open the exact thumbnail you chose; closing the viewer restores your position.
- Separate timeline and thumbnail scroll positions while the page remains open.
- Gold hover elevation and clear video/carousel badges.
- Gold and Graphite studio themes, remembered in the current browser.

## Watch and swipe

- Mixed photo/video carousels with horizontal touch swipes, mouse drags, and thumbnail selection.
- Left advances; right returns to the previous item. Vertical gestures still scroll the wall.
- Desktop theater with full captions, media navigation, keyboard controls, and a clear Close button.
- Advancing beyond the viewer's final carousel item returns to the wall.
- On the ordinary mobile wall, media stays inline with native video controls. Thumbnail mode opens its selected item on both mobile and desktop.
- Playback follows the visible video or selected carousel item; desktop hover takes priority.
- One active local video avoids competing sound. Leaving view, changing media, hiding the tab, or casting stops local playback as appropriate.
- A transparent speaker control; sound is requested by default and explicit mute is respected.

Audible autoplay remains controlled by the browser and device. A first tap can be necessary. Physical receiver/phone compatibility must be checked on the device being used.

## Build a favorites list and cast it

- Give a heart to a post, or use a star to save one individual photo/video.
- Save directly from thumbnails to assemble a presentation quickly.
- Reorder favorites and remove missing items.
- Play a local presentation with timed photos, complete videos, pause/resume, and optional repeat.
- Cast one selected item from the wall, theater, or favorites viewer using an overlay icon.
- Changing the carousel selection updates the media on the TV while casting that card.
- Cast an ordered mixed-media favorites queue. The receiver advances through the queue and can repeat it.
- TV play/pause and stop controls; local playback pauses to avoid duplicate audio.
- Media-only Google Cast: the TV receives media/slides, not the webpage or admin interface.
- Photos become cached, silent still-image MP4 slides; videos use compatible H.264/AAC playback copies.
- Correct public HTTPS origin selection behind tunnels, receiver error messages, byte-range streaming, and media CORS headers.

Favorites belong to the current browser and hostname. They do not sync between phones and computers. Google Cast requires supported Chrome, HTTPS, a compatible TV/Chromecast, network discovery, and receiver access to the media. Chrome on iOS does not support this sender SDK. See [casting instructions](FAVORITES-CASTING.md).

## Manage your own CMS

- Username/password login with a signed HTTP-only session cookie.
- Unique initial credentials generated by setup; password hashes stored in the database after account initialization.
- Drag-and-drop JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4, and MOV uploads.
- Multiple files become an ordered carousel.
- Editable timestamps let you backdate a memory into its correct place.
- Caption editing and in-place wall editing for logged-in administrators.
- Delete a post and its original/derived local media.
- Customize the header title, small introductory heading, and description.
- Change your username and password from Admin.
- Visitor/post/media/heart counts, approximate city/country, device, OS, browser, and recent visits.
- Local/private IPs are not shown; IP-derived city estimates can be absent or inaccurate.
- Clear all visitor statistics when needed.
- An Info panel containing installation, feature, operations, admin, and casting documentation.

## Preserve and operate

- Import your own extracted Instagram export without supplying an Instagram password.
- Preserve export timestamps, captions, carousel order, and local originals.
- Repair common double-encoded UTF-8 caption text.
- Local SQLite database and filesystem uploads, with thumbnail/video processing.
- Source-only release ZIP and tarball downloads with SHA-256 checksums.
- Start, stop, status, rebuild, and restart commands with port-conflict protection.
- Debian systemd services that start the application and tunnel at boot.
- Cloudflare Quick Tunnel for temporary HTTPS; named tunnel and custom domain for a stable address.
- Private backup/migration instructions and a previous-build fallback during systemd rebuilds.
- MIT-licensed application source.

You maintain the server, storage, backups, and access policy. The release is empty: it includes no personal collection, account database, visitor history, or credentials. The published gallery is public; the management interface requires authentication.
